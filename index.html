We value honesty, innovation, teamwork, and continuous improvement. We work to meet customer needs while creating long-term value for our customers, employees, and community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to produce and deliver safe, high-quality food products that meet customer expectations and applicable food safety requirements.

We are committed to responsible sourcing, hygienic production, quality control, and continuous improvement throughout our operations.

Our Vision

To become a respected and trusted food brand in Ethiopia and international markets by providing quality products, maintaining high food safety standards, and building lasting relationships with our customers.

Quality & Food Safety

At Golden Honey Foods Industry, quality and food safety are essential to our production operations. We focus on hygienic processing, proper packaging, safe storage, and consistent product quality.

Quality Standards

  • HACCP: Identifying and controlling food safety hazards.
  • GMP: Good Manufacturing Practices for hygienic production.
  • ISO 22000: A framework for food safety management systems.
  • Quality Control: Checking raw materials, processing, packaging, and finished products.
  • Food Safety: Proper handling, cleaning, and storage procedures.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide products that meet defined quality requirements and applicable food safety regulations.
